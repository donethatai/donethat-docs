This is the profile your followers and group members see. By default it is private. You can change that in [privacy settings](/settings/privacy).

People you accept as followers always see your activity. With Private visibility they are the only ones who do; see [groups](/groups) for what your groups see at each level.

If you set up a handle and make your profile public, you get a public URL to share. You can choose whether it is indexed on Google.

You can edit your profile picture, name, handle, title, and social links in your [settings](/settings).

Add a handle to your profile to get a sharable link, and add a name so that others can find you when looking for people to follow. To look for people yourself, use Find people under Following in the sidebar. You can set your profile visibility in [settings](/settings).

Your title is meant like a job title, anything that allows others to better understand who you are when looking for people to follow or checking out your profile.
