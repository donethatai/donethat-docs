You can manage your subscription in Stripe by following the "Manage Subscription" button in your [settings](/settings) page.

By default, all subscriptions are individual and can be upgraded to an organization subscription.

After subscribing, you will receive an email three days before the trial ends as a reminder.

To earn referral bonuses, create your referral code in [settings](/settings). Your friends can use this code during checkout and both you and your friend will get a free month of DoneThat.
