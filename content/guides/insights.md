Insights lets you ask your own questions about your work and keep the answers as cards. Each card is a chart you set up once and that stays current. Drag cards to reorder them, and use "New insight" to add one.

## Building an insight

Pick what to measure:

- **Tracked time**: the time DoneThat has a record of.
- **Mood**: the moods you tracked. Don can check in on your mood to make this easier, see [Don settings](/settings/don).
- **Break time**: time between your first and last activity of a day that nothing accounts for.
- **Task switches**: how often you move from one task to another.
- **Longest focus stretch**: your longest unbroken run on one task per day, averaged over the days with data.

Then choose how to show it (total time, average per day, percent of tracked time, value of time or a count), the period (last week, month, quarter or year) and how to split it: by category, project, activity, place, home or office, goal, work hours, hour of day, weekday or mood, or over time. You can split by two of these at once.

Value of time uses the hourly value you set in your [profile settings](/settings/profile).

## Comparing

Compare against the previous period to see what changed, or against a group of people:

- **Just me**: only your own data.
- **A group** you are in.
- **Your organization**.
- **DoneThat users**: everyone on DoneThat who shares publicly.

Comparisons with other people are always anonymous averages. Only people whose [visibility](/settings/privacy) reaches that audience are counted: Organization or above for a group or your organization, and Public for all DoneThat users. A figure is only shown when at least five people have data for it, so no one person's time can be read from it. Value of time is never compared with other people.

## Availability

Insights is switched on per account. If the page says it is not enabled for your account yet, [contact us](mailto:support@donethat.ai) to try it.
