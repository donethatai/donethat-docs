Insights lets you ask your own questions about your work and keep the answers as cards. Each card is a chart you set up once and that stays current. Drag cards to reorder them, and use "New insight" to add one.

## Building an insight

Pick what to measure:

- **Tracked time**: the time DoneThat has a record of.
- **Mood**: the moods you tracked. Don can check in on your mood to make this easier, see [Don settings](/settings/don).
- **Break time**: time between your first and last activity of a day that nothing accounts for.
- **Task switches**: how often you move from one task to another.
- **Longest focus stretch**: your longest unbroken run on one task per day, averaged over the days with data.

Then choose how to show it (total time, average per day, percent of tracked time, value of time, or how many), the period (last week, month, quarter or year) and how to split it: by category, project, activity, place, home or office, goal, work hours, hour of day, weekday or mood, or over time. You can split by two of these at once.

Value of time uses the hourly value you set in your [profile settings](/settings/profile).

## Whose data, and comparing

Choose whose data the chart shows:

- **Just me**: only your own data.
- **A group** you are in.
- **Your organization**.
- **DoneThat users**: everyone active on DoneThat.

Then optionally compare it: with the previous period to see what changed, or with one of the others above, for example your own time against your group's.

Figures about other people are always anonymous averages, and only shown when at least five people have data for them, so no one person's time can be read from them. For a group or your organization, they count the people whose [visibility](/settings/privacy) is Organization or above. DoneThat users counts everyone active, anonymously. Value of time is never compared with other people.

## Availability

Insights is switched on per account. If the page says it is not enabled for your account yet, [contact us](mailto:support@donethat.ai) to try it.
