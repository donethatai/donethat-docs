While you have no summary yet, the setup checklist is the first tile on [home](/summaries). Everywhere else, and on home once your first summary is in, it floats above the menu at the bottom of the left sidebar as a "Get set up" pill showing how many steps you have done. Click it to open the checklist panel. With the sidebar collapsed it shows as a progress ring.

The panel expands the next step you haven't done yet and collapses the rest, so there is only ever one thing in front of you. Every step except waiting for your first summary has a button that takes you where you need to go.

## The steps

1. **Install the desktop app**: DoneThat tracks your work from the app running in your tray. This step completes once the app first checks in.
2. **Review capture permissions**: decide which data DoneThat may use. "Open permissions" opens the desktop app's Setup section, or [permissions settings](/settings/permissions) in a browser. Ticks itself about five minutes after the first capture is processed.
3. **Review your recording time**: check which days and hours DoneThat records so it matches how you actually work. Already complete if you have set non-default work hours; otherwise it completes when you open recording settings.
4. **Create your projects**: projects group your work so summaries read the way you'd describe them. Completes when you have at least one project.
5. **Set up your profile**: add your name and handle so shared summaries show who they belong to.
6. **Set your first goal**: goals turn tracked time into progress you can review. Goals live on the summaries page.
7. **Connect your apps**: link your calendar, Slack or Discord. Already complete if you have one connected; otherwise it completes when you open integrations settings.
8. **Wait for your first summary**: press "Finish day" to review your data when you are done, or wait for the automatic closing in the early morning. This is the only step you can't finish by clicking something.
9. **Create or join a group**: see what others are working on, based on your privacy settings. The button opens the create group dialog. Completes once you are in a group, whether you created it or joined one.

Steps tick themselves. There is nothing to check off by hand, and nothing to undo if you change something later.

## Hiding it

"Hide this checklist" at the bottom of the panel removes it from the sidebar. Your progress is kept, and you can bring it back from **Settings → Account**, where a "Show again" card appears while the checklist is hidden but unfinished.

The checklist also retires itself once every step is done, and it follows your account rather than your browser, so hiding it on one machine hides it everywhere.

## Don and the checklist

Don reads the same progress the checklist does, so he won't email you about a step you have already finished. The first two steps are the exception in the other direction: Don only starts working once the app is installed and capturing, so he never nudges you about those.
