The organization serves for combining groups and for billing. Everybody can only be member of one organization but of many groups.

Organization admins can set a picture for the organization in its general settings.

If you set a domain for your organization, people joining DoneThat with that domain will automatically be added to your organization.

New members of the organization join its default groups: at first the group created with the organization. Admins choose them under New members in the organization's group settings, and can switch them all off so that joining the organization adds nobody to a group. Billing counts organization members, whichever groups they are in.

You can access your invoices by clicking on "Manage Subscription". 

When people join or leave an organization, the seat billing automatically adjusts.

To invite people to your organization or a group, add their email in its settings. In a group that belongs to your organization, people already in your organization are added right away, and people in another organization can only be invited when the group is public. Anyone else is invited to your organization and joins that group when they accept. A group without an organization always sends an invitation.
